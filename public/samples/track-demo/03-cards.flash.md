# Night Sky — Flashcards

```flash id:why-stars-twinkle
Why do stars twinkle?
---
They are effectively **point sources**. Moving pockets of air bend the single
incoming ray differently from moment to moment, and every deflection shows up
as a flicker.
```

```flash id:why-planets-steady
Why do planets shine steadily?
---
They arrive as a small **disc** rather than a point. Many neighbouring rays are
bent in different directions at once, so the distortions average out.
```

```flash id:nightly-vs-seasonal
Which motion makes the sky wheel overhead during one night?
---
The Earth's **spin on its axis**. The Earth's **orbit** is what changes which
constellations are visible from season to season.
```

```flash id:retrograde
What is retrograde motion?
---
The apparent backtracking of a planet against the fixed stars for a few weeks,
caused by the Earth overtaking it (or being overtaken) as both orbit the Sun.
```
